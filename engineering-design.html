Engineering & Design

I deliver professional electrical engineering and design solutions focused on safety, efficiency, reliability, and practical performance. Combining technical expertise with hands-on industry experience, I specialize in electrical system planning, circuit design, control panel layouts, and power distribution solutions.

From initial concepts to detailed engineering documentation, my goal is to develop reliable, cost-effective, and well-structured electrical systems that meet project requirements and applicable industry standards.

Core Expertise

  • Electrical System Design - Single Line Diagrams (SLD) and electrical schematics, Electrical load calculation and distribution planning, Power system design for industrial and residential applications, Generator and Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) integration.
  • Control Circuit Design - DOL, Star-Delta, and Forward-Reverse motor control circuits, Motor starter and protection circuit design, Control panel wiring diagrams and layouts, Electrical automation and control system planning.
  • Panel & Power Distribution - Electrical control panel design and component selection, Distribution board (DB) planning and layout, Protection coordination and equipment rating selection, Efficient power distribution and system reliability.
  • Project Engineering - Electrical project planning and technical documentation, Material and equipment specifications, Installation and implementation support, Safety-focused engineering practices and quality control.

Key Features of My Services

  • Safety First: Protection-focused designs using appropriate MCBs, RCCBs/ELCBs, overload relays, and other protective devices.
  • Reliable Performance: Practical engineering solutions designed for long-term operational stability.
  • Energy Efficiency: Optimized electrical layouts and power distribution to minimize energy losses.
  • Technical Documentation: Clear diagrams, circuit layouts, and engineering specifications.
  • Practical Implementation: Designs developed with real-world installation and maintenance requirements in mind.

Why Choose My Engineering & Design Services?

  • Strong technical foundation in Electrical Engineering.
  • Hands-on experience in industrial electrical maintenance and installation.
  • Practical knowledge of motor control, generator systems, and electrical panels.
  • Understanding of industrial, commercial, and residential electrical applications.
  • Commitment to safety, precision, quality, and continuous improvement.
