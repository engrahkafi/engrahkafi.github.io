Residential Wiring

I provide comprehensive and reliable residential wiring solutions designed to ensure safety, efficiency, and long-term comfort. Combining electrical safety codes with precise craftsmanship, I design and implement reliable electrical circuits for modern residences.

From new home electrical installation to complete renovation wiring, my focus is on delivering secure, future-ready, and energy-efficient electrical systems that protect families and property.

Core Expertise

  • Whole-House Wiring & Layout - Complete circuit design, conduit layout, light point distribution, and power outlet placement for residential buildings and villas.
  • Consumer Unit & Distribution Board - Installation and proper balancing of Main Switch, MCB, RCCB/ELCB, and surge protection devices (SPD) to eliminate fire and shock hazards.
  • Smart Home Automation & Lighting - Modern LED architecture, smart switches, dimmer modules, security sensor lighting, and energy-saving lighting control.
  • Earthing & Grounding Systems - Low-resistance earth pit design, grounding electrodes, and lightning protection to guarantee resident safety.

Key Features of My Services

  • Shock & Fire Protection: Use of calibrated RCCB/GFCI devices with accurate trip sensitivity for maximum safety.
  • Balanced Phase Loads: Uniform distribution across residential phases to avoid voltage dips and breaker tripping.
  • Clean & Concealed Conduit Work: Neat, hidden conduit routing with premium finish flush to interior design standards.
  • Dedicated High-Power Circuits: Dedicated wiring lines for AC units, water heaters (geysers), ovens, and water pumps.
  • Clear Circuit Labeling: Distinct panel marking and schematic reference for straightforward maintenance.

Why Choose My Residential Wiring Services?

  • Certified in National Skill Certificate – I in Electrical Installation and Maintenance (Civil Construction).
  • Up-to-date knowledge of modern building codes, wiring standards, and high-efficiency materials.
  • Clean, neat, and highly organized wiring layouts that blend seamlessly with home aesthetics.
  • Rigorous pre-occupancy testing including insulation, continuity, and ground fault checks.
  • Reliable advice on energy savings and solar or backup UPS/inverter integration.
