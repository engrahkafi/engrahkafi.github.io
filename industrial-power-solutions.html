Industrial Power Solutions

I provide trusted and efficient electrical engineering services with a focus on reliability, safety, and industrial performance. Combining technical expertise with hands-on factory floor experience, I handle system overhauls, routine upkeep, and industrial plant electrification.

From initial diagnosis to complete system commissioning, my goal is to ensure maximum uptime, optimal energy distribution, and adherence to international safety codes.

Core Expertise

  • Main Distribution & Substation - Main Distribution Board (MDB), Sub-Distribution Board (SDB) setup, voltage & current monitoring, phase balancing, and busbar trunking.
  • Generator & ATS Integration - Generator synchronization, Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) commissioning, routine filter replacement, and load testing.
  • Motor Starters & Heavy Drives - Star-Delta, Direct-On-Line (DOL), Soft Starter, and VFD wiring with thermal overload and short-circuit protection.
  • Preventive Maintenance & Safety - Thermal imaging of panels, power quality analysis, insulation resistance testing, and emergency shutdown integration.

Key Features of My Services

  • Zero-Downtime Focus: Planned scheduled maintenance routines to prevent unexpected factory production halts.
  • Safety First: Complete protection coordination using circuit breakers, ground fault relays, and surge arresters.
  • Industrial Compliance: All installations align with BNBC, NFPA, and IEC industrial wiring codes.
  • Comprehensive Reports: Detailed inspection logs, test readings, and preventive maintenance guides for plant managers.
  • Emergency Support: Prompt troubleshooting and rapid resolution for industrial power trips and equipment breakdowns.

Why Choose My Industrial Power Solutions?

  • Direct field experience maintaining factory distribution boards and heavy machinery.
  • Proven track record in high-voltage and low-voltage electrical panel servicing.
  • Thorough understanding of electrical safety and personal protective equipment (PPE) protocols.
  • Hands-on troubleshooting of automated industrial control circuits.
  • Dedication to durable engineering and long-term industrial efficiency.
